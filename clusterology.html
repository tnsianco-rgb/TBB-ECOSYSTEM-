From numbers to structure

One universe. Five families. One connected structure.

The UK49S number universe contains 49 numbers. TBB Clusterology organises that universe into five defined families, then examines relationships within each family through Odd and Even teams and structured wheel formations.

The purpose is to replace scattered number handling with a consistent mathematical framework that can be studied, understood and applied over time.

Internal organisation

Odd and Even teams

Within each family, numbers are organised into Odd and Even teams. This creates a further layer of order for examining number relationships and building structured formations.

Clusters are nets. The numbers are fish. UK49S is the ocean.
